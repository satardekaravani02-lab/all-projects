GURU PURNIMA

Guru Purnima is a festival celebrated to honor and thank teachers, parents, and spiritual guides. It is observed on the full moon day (Purnima) in the Hindu month of Ashadha (June–July). The day is dedicated to Vyasa, so it is also called Vyasa Purnima.

Importance of Guru Purnima

  • It shows respect and gratitude to teachers and mentors
  • It reminds us that teachers help us gain knowledge and wisdom.
  • It encourages us to learn, grow, and become better people.
  • It teaches the values of respect, discipline, and kindness.

If you cannot meet your Guru in person, simply calling or sending a heartfelt message to your teacher or mentor is considered an act of gratitude. Charity is also regarded as highly auspicious on Guru Purnima. Donating books, food, clothes or educational materials is believed to bring the blessings of Guru (Jupiter) and promote wisdom, prosperity and spiritual progress.Guru Purnima is a special day to thank our teachers and guides for helping us learn and succeed in life. It reminds us that a good teacher can change our future through knowledge and guidance.
